Race City 3D — 3D model and HDRI credits
========================================

Cars
----
- Ferrari 458 Italia glTF (ferrari.glb)
  From the three.js r160 examples (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js).
  Used as the sports-car / Tesla Roadster stand-in.

- Car Concept (CarConcept.glb) — CC-BY 4.0
  Khronos glTF Sample Assets.
  Based on a public-domain (CC0) concept-car model by Unity Fan on Sketchfab,
  cleaned and packaged by Khronos.
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept
  Used as the sedan / SUV / pickup / police stand-in (recolored, scaled).

- Cesium Milk Truck (CesiumMilkTruck.glb) — CC-BY 4.0
  Khronos glTF Sample Assets / Cesium.
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CesiumMilkTruck
  Used as a truck / semi stand-in (trailer added in-game for semis).

HDRIs (Poly Haven, CC0)
-----------------------
Bundled with three.js r160 examples/textures/equirectangular:
- venice_sunset_1k.hdr
- royal_esplanade_1k.hdr
- moonless_golf_1k.hdr
Originals: https://polyhaven.com/ (CC0)

Semis, Cybertruck, monster truck, fire truck, and trains stay as
higher-detail procedural meshes when a matching CC0/CC-BY glTF was not available.
