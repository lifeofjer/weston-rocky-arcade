by Weston and Rocky · for ages 8–9 (Weston is 7 and saving up!)

You are the orange tabby with green eyes. Walk a big colorful city. Start with a tiny house. Buy a rusty $1 car, then save 20 coins for a nicer used coupe. Ride city buses, shop inside Bullseye Mart with a cart, and when you buy a bigger house a Haul-It truck helps you move. The Palm Villa has a swimming pool and supercars in the garage. No weapons — just cat life!

HOW IT'S BUILT

A few game-dev ideas, said simply:

Saving is a superpower. Coins stay in a tiny notebook on this iPad (localStorage). Gold coins refill around the city, so you can always earn more. Save 20 for the used coupe before the fancy cars. Old saves still load!

Better tools earn faster. A rusty car can deliver pizza. The $20 used coupe is nicer and quicker. A supercar finishes the same job even faster. The Palm Villa even comes with garage supercars.

City life. Sliding doors open when you walk near them. Push a shopping cart, pay the cashier, ride a bus, and a Haul-It truck moves your furniture when you buy a new house.

Play Together. A WebSocket room shares where each cat and car is so two screens match.
