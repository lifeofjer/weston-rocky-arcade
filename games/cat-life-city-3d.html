HOW IT'S BUILT

A few game-dev ideas, said simply:

Saving is a superpower. Coins stay in a tiny notebook on this iPad (localStorage). If you spend everything, you cannot buy the cool car — so save a little, then splurge!

Better tools earn faster. A rusty car can deliver pizza. A supercar finishes the same job quicker, so you earn more. That is strategy: buy the next step, not always the fanciest thing first.

Soft-body dents. Crash and vertices near the hit fold in, just like Race City. The rusty $1 car already looks crumpled.

Play Together. A WebSocket room shares where each cat and car is so two screens match.
