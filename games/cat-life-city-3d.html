by Weston and Rocky · for ages 8–9 (Weston is 7 and saving up!)

You are the orange tabby with green eyes. Walk a big colorful city. Start with a tiny house that has real stairs to a second floor. HOW IT'S BUILT

A few game-dev ideas, said simply:

Saving is a superpower. Coins stay in a tiny notebook on this iPad (localStorage). Gold coins refill around the city, so you can always earn more. Every car costs 20 coins — rusty, used coupe, or the fanciest supercar. Old saves still load!

Better tools earn faster. A rusty car can deliver pizza. The used coupe is nicer and quicker. A supercar finishes the same job even faster. The Palm Villa even comes with garage supercars.

City life. Sliding doors open when you walk near them. Push a shopping cart, pay the cashier, ride a bus, and a Haul-It truck moves your furniture when you buy a new house. Walk upstairs on real stairs in the starter house and Palm Villa.

How fire trucks find a fire. They do not fly. They follow the roads, turn at corners, and drive to the smoke — just like following a map. Slow down at corners so you do not crash!

Volt Sky Roadster. Tap FLY (or Y) to hover. CLIMB / DIVE (or E / Q) change altitude. GRIP (or G) sticks you to a building wall so you can drive on it. Jump or turn grip off to drop back to the street.

Play Together. A WebSocket room shares where each cat and car is so two screens match.

Christmas. From Nov 20 to Dec 31 (Denver time) the city snows and snowplows clear the roads. At home: decorate, bake, build a snowman, hang lights, leave milk and cookies, then sleep — presents on Christmas morning! Use ?theme=none to turn it off.
