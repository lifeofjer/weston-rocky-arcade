HOW IT'S BUILT

A few flying ideas, said simply:

Speed plus lift. Air rushing over the wings pushes the plane up. That is why you taxi fast down the runway before you pull up. Slow planes sink. Fast planes fly!

Landing is the opposite. Slow down, stay level, and kiss the runway. A bouncy landing still counts. A super-soft landing pays bonus coins.

Arcade auto-level. Let go of the stick and the plane gently flattens out, so loops and rolls are fun — not scary.

Saving is a superpower. Coins and unlocked planes stay in a tiny notebook on this iPad (localStorage).

Play Together. A WebSocket room shares where each cat-plane is so two screens match.
