RACE CITY 3D

by Weston and Rocky · for ages 8–9 (Weston is 7 and getting fast!)

Open-world racing like Asphalt + Fortnite. Pick a map, then a car. The white Tesla Roadster can FLY as long as you want (press F to toggle) and has rockets that never run out. In the air: climb, dive, steer, and flip/roll. Press F again to glide — land on your wheels to keep driving. Crash, become the orange tabby, and shop at Target with pretend coins.

Hint: boost on straights, brake before corners.

HOW IT'S BUILT

A few game-dev ideas, said simply:

Shiny real cars

Each car is a 3D mesh with PBR paint — metalness + clearcoat — so the sky reflects on the body, just like a real photo. Wheels have rims, tires, brake discs, glass, lights, and mirrors. Same car kit as Rail City 3D (no holes in the body).

Crashes like BeamNG

Soft-body damage: when you hit something we push the vertices near the impact point. Panels crumple, hoods fly off, glass shatters. Off-center hits spin you. Big hits can roll you (tall trucks roll more). Train hits plow you — no wheel kick.

Keeping iPad smooth

LOD + instancing: far-away cars become simpler shapes, and the game lowers extra shine if frames drop. On iPad, fly/climb/dive/steer/flip buttons sit on the right so you can fly without a keyboard.

Flying the Roadster

F toggles rockets on. While flying, the car holds altitude until you climb, dive, or turn fly off. Then it glides. A wheels-down landing keeps driving; a bad landing can still crash.

Car models

Real glTF cars, credited in the repo: Ferrari 458 from three.js examples (sports / Roadster stand-in), Khronos Car Concept CC-BY (sedan / SUV / pickup), Cesium Milk Truck CC-BY (truck / semi cab). Crash damage still crumples the actual body mesh from the hit point. Semis, Cybertruck, monster, and fire truck stay procedural when no matching CC0/CC-BY mesh fit.

City lighting

Poly Haven CC0 HDRIs (via three.js) light the cars so paint and glass actually reflect the sky. Soft shadows, a little bloom, and haze make the blocks feel farther away.

Play Together
